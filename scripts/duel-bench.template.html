What the engine actually does, measured

Two hundred complete matches stepped through the real simulation, counting every frame. This is not an opinion about the fight — it is what the fight spends its time doing.

50.8smedian match Range 26.5–72s. The hero slot is on screen while somebody reads a page; almost nobody watches one to the end.
62%frames doing nothing Standing in guard, advancing, circling or backstepping. Nearly two thirds of the fight is the gap between exchanges.
12%frames striking Every attack in the move table added together — rising, overhead, diagonal, thrust and leap.
1.9%frames in hit-stop The freeze on contact that makes a blow read as a blow. It is the whole impact budget, and it is very small.

All thirty-five modules do run, so the choreography is not repeating itself — the problem is density, not variety. If what you are seeing is “two figures standing about”, that is fault 3, and these numbers say you are right.

“The battles are still lacking” — which one is it?

Every one of these is a different fix in a different part of the engine, and they pull against each other, so guessing wrong makes the fight worse. Reply with the numbers that match what you are watching. More than one is fine, and “none of these, it’s ___” is the most useful answer of all.

  1. 1 · repetitiveThe same exchanges keep coming round. You start recognising a move before it happens.
  2. 2 · no impactBlades meet and nothing happens. Hits don’t feel like they cost anything.
  3. 3 · too slowFloaty. They drift rather than commit, and the pauses between exchanges are dead air.
  4. 4 · too busyConstant motion, no rhythm. Nothing lands because nothing is ever still.
  5. 5 · can’t read itAt ornament size it’s two smudges. You can’t tell who is who or what just happened.
  6. 6 · no stakesIt never builds. Somebody dies and it didn’t feel like the fight was going anywhere.
  7. 7 · bad footworkThey slide, skate or stand in the wrong place. The feet don’t sell the weight.
  8. 8 · not a swordfightThe individual moves are fine but it doesn’t look like two people fighting each other.
